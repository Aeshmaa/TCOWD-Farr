## Yima, the Hollow King (gold)
> Colossal ancient Persian Achaemenid king, 7x human height, his body a hollow cracked bronze-and-stone shell with large missing pieces revealing an empty dark interior and a faint dying gold ember in the chest. Long regal sorrowful gaunt face, long braided curled Achaemenid beard, empty eye sockets with tiny gold pinpoints. Tall cracked crenellated Persian crown. Rotting tattered royal pleated robes with faded gold trim. Holds a tarnished golden ring and a long gold-inlaid royal goad. Massive chains bolting him to colossal foundation pillars. Streams of amber-gold light pulled from glowing crystal veins and tiny adventurers flowing into his hollow chest and leaking out of his cracks. High above, out of reach, a radiant golden falcon of light flies away as he reaches up toward it. Vast underground cavern, Persepolis double-bull capital pillars, walls carved with glowing seal glyphs. Palette: tarnished bronze, cold stone grey, warm gold accents. Tragic, melancholic, menacing.

## The Orphan Process (cyan)
> Colossal asymmetrical humanoid war machine, 7x human height, assembled from many hijacked smaller mechs, frames and drones welded and fused together, visible hulls and cockpits grafted into its body, sleek gun-metal grey and white plating corrupted and scavenged. Exposed cracked processor core in the chest glowing sickly cyan-white with glitch artifacts, liquid data-light dripping. Smooth featureless faceplate with a single vertical cyan slit-eye, cracked to show corrupted glyphs. A crude crown of looted ancient Persian stone and gold fragments bolted to its head. Dozens of glowing cyan cables from its fingers and back like puppet strings controlling smaller mechs and drones whose lights have turned cyan. One arm a massive cannon-claw built from fused weapons. Graveyard of crashed colony-ark wreckage among ruined Persepolis double-bull columns, amber crystal veins, smoke, sparks, cold fog, sky dome cracked like glass. Palette: steel grey, white, black, sickly cyan accent, orange sparks. Cold, oppressive, inhuman.
